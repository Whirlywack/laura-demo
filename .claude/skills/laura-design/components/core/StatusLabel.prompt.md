Shows whether a tablet was taken, as a marker plus plain words; use it on every medication row.

```jsx
<StatusLabel status="taken" />
<StatusLabel status="earlier" />
<StatusLabel status="not-confirmed" />
<StatusLabel status="not-taken" />
```

Filled burgundy = taken on the call; outlined burgundy = she says she took it earlier; dashed grey = not confirmed yet; solid black with bold text = not taken.
