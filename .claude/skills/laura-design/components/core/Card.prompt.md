Rounded surface that holds every block on a Laura page; pick the tone by meaning, not decoration.

```jsx
<Card tone="action">…the carer's to-do…</Card>
<Card tone="quiet" compact>…folded care plan row…</Card>
```

- `tone="action"` (straw) appears at most once per screen.
- `tone="live"` (petrol) is only for a call in progress or an item that just arrived on marketing pages.
